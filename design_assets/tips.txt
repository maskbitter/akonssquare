App Design Assets - Ultra Modern 2026 Style

1. Android Launcher Icons:
   Place the XML files in the 'res/drawable' folder of your Android project.
   In AndroidManifest.xml, set:
   android:icon="@mipmap/ic_launcher"
   android:roundIcon="@mipmap/ic_launcher_round" (pointing to an adaptive-icon XML)

2. Flutter Splash Screen:
   Use the 'ModernLogoPainter' class provided in 'splash_screen_logo.dart' with a CustomPaint widget.
   Standard size: 120x120 or 80x80 for a cleaner look.

3. Typography Recommendation:
   Main Title: AKONS SQUARE
   Font: Extra Bold / Black (w900)
   Letter Spacing: 5.0
   Color: White (0.9 opacity) with subtle shadow.

4. Color Palette:
   Primary: #00FFC2 (Electric Cyan)
   Secondary: #00D2FF (Sky Blue)
   Background: Deep Radial Gradient (#24292E to #0A0C0E)
